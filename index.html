About Us

Welcome to SmoKehouse

SmoKehouse started with a simple idea: great barbecue needs patience. Our meats are seasoned by hand, smoked slowly over charcoal, and finished on the grill so every bite is tender and full of flavor.

From weekday lunches to family dinners, we keep the menu honest and the portions generous, and we cook everything fresh.
